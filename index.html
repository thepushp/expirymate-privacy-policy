ExpiryMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

ExpiryMate ("the app") helps you track expiry dates for food, medicine and household products. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: your product list stays on your phone, and we do not collect it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. ExpiryMate has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. The developer has no server that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

The products, expiry dates, batches, quantities, notes, categories, reminder settings and history you enter are stored in a database on your phone only. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only when you choose to scan a barcode or read a printed expiry date. Barcodes and printed text are read on your phone. Photos taken for reading text are processed on your device, are not uploaded, and are deleted straight away. If you pick a photo from your gallery, it is read on your phone in the same way and is not uploaded or saved. Camera access is optional: you can always add products by typing them in.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They do not use a server or push service, and you can change or turn them off in the app or in your phone settings.

Barcode lookup (Open Food Facts)

When you scan or enter a barcode, the app may look it up in Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free, open database, to fill in the product name, brand and picture. This lookup sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, along with your device's IP address (which any internet request reveals) and the app name and version. It does not send your product list, expiry dates or any other personal data. Product pictures are loaded from Open Food Facts servers. Open Food Facts handles that data under its own privacy policy at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy. The lookup is optional and the app works without an internet connection.

Backups and exports

You can export your data as a JSON or CSV file and import a backup later. Exported files are created on your phone and go only where you choose to share or save them. We never receive them.

Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we do not have it. The only external service the app contacts is Open Food Facts, as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because all data is on your phone, you are in control of it. You can edit or delete products in the app, export a copy, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
